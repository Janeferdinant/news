Results

Key Findings

  • In this synthetic sample (seed 42, 10 per group), the AI group's mean gain was 4.3 points and the control group's was 2.0, so the AI group's gain was greater. This only reflects the values the script was told to generate.
  • Further study is needed to confirm results in larger samples.

Discussion

Consider experiment design, fairness, and reproducibility when evaluating educational interventions.
